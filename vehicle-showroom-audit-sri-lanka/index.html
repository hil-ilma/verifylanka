What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Showroom welcome

Greeting, waiting time and attention to the customer.

02

Sales advice

Whether needs are asked about, and honest information about models and prices is given.

03

Test drive offer

Whether a test drive is offered and arranged well.

04

Price and finance clarity

Whether the total cost, taxes and finance terms are explained.

05

Service booking

Ease of booking and accuracy of time and cost estimates.

06

Workshop experience

Waiting area, explanation of work done and billing.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Buyers visit several showrooms before choosing, and sales conversations differ widely between dealers. After the sale, the workshop decides whether they come back. Both can be checked quietly, one site at a time.

How it works

From brief to report

Auditors visit as buyers and as service customers, following set scenarios. We score each dealer and workshop, and report on how they compare to your standard and to competitors if you want.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.
