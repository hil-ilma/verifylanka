Mystery Shopper Programs for Retail, Service and Brand Operations in Sri Lanka

A mystery shopper program is a planned series of visits, calls or orders, run to the same checklist across all your outlets. We design the program around your business, whether you run shops, service counters, a franchise or a dealer network.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

One visit tells you about one day. A program tells you about your business. When every outlet is checked the same way, on a schedule, you can compare branches fairly, reward the good ones and fix the weak ones with facts.

How it works

From brief to report

We agree the outlets, the scenarios and how often to visit. Our mystery shoppers follow the same checklist at every location and we send one scored report. Programs can run once, every quarter or every month.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

What is the difference between a mystery shopper, a secret shopper and a mystery guest?

They are the same idea with different names. A mystery shopper or secret shopper visits a shop or branch. A mystery guest stays at a hotel or eats at a restaurant. A mystery caller tests a phone line. All of them act as normal customers and report what happened.

How many visits does a program need?

It depends on how many outlets you have and how often you want a result. Many programs start with one visit per outlet, then repeat monthly or quarterly.

Can one program cover the whole island?

Yes. We cover every district of Sri Lanka, so city and rural outlets are measured in the same program.
