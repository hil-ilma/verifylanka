Branch Audit in Sri Lanka

Head office sees averages. Customers see one branch. Our branch audits visit each location as a customer and rank them, so you can copy what the best ones do and fix the weakest.

What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Branch appearance

Signage, entrance, lighting, cleanliness, brand display and condition of furniture.

02

Staff conduct

Uniform, greeting, attitude and how staff handle a busy period.

03

Process accuracy

Whether forms, fees, documents and steps are explained and done correctly.

04

Waiting times

Arrival to service time and how the queue is managed.

05

Notices and compliance

Required notices, price lists, licences and safety signs in place and up to date.

06

Customer facilities

Seating, washrooms, parking, accessibility and customer comfort.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Two branches of the same company can feel like different businesses. The difference is usually the manager and the team on the day. A branch audit makes that visible, so good branches get recognised and weak ones get help.

How it works

From brief to report

We build one score sheet and use it at every branch. Each branch gets a score, a rank and specific notes. You can repeat the audit every month or quarter to see who is improving.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Can you audit all branches island wide at the same time?

Yes. Auditors in every region visit within the same window, so the comparison between branches is fair.

Can we track improvement over time?

Yes. Many clients repeat the audit monthly or quarterly. See our monthly retainer audits.

Do you share results with each branch?

Only if you want us to. By default, results go to the people you name at head office.
