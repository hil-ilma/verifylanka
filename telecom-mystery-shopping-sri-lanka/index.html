What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Plan and package advice

Whether staff recommend a plan that fits the customer, and explain charges clearly.

02

SIM, port in and activation

Process speed, document checks and accuracy.

03

Fault and complaint handling

How staff deal with slow internet, billing disputes and device problems.

04

Upselling honesty

Whether add ons are explained, or quietly added.

05

Dealer and franchise outlets

Whether authorised dealers match your brand and service standard.

06

Hotline experience

Call pick up time, script use and whether the fault is logged.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Telecom customers can switch providers with a SIM swap. Experience at the outlet and on the hotline decides whether they stay. Dealer outlets are hard to monitor, and they are often where service standards slip.

How it works

From brief to report

We set scenarios such as buying a data package, reporting a fault or asking about fibre. Auditors visit your own stores and dealers across the island, and call your hotline, then score each against your standard.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.
