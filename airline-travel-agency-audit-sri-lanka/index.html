Travel customers spend a lot and remember mistakes. We check how your booking offices, agents and call centres explain fares, changes and refunds.

What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Booking enquiry

Whether the customer is offered clear options with correct prices.

02

Fee and fare explanation

Whether taxes, baggage and change fees are explained.

03

Change and refund handling

How staff deal with a customer who wants to change or cancel.

04

Office service

Greeting, waiting time and presentation at counters.

05

Call centre

Pick up time, accuracy and tone on the phone.

06

Visa and document guidance

Whether staff give correct information or point to official sources.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Travel problems are expensive and stressful. Most disputes start with unclear fees or a promise that staff did not keep. Checking from the customer side exposes the gaps before the trip.

How it works

From brief to report

Auditors run enquiry and change scenarios in person and by phone, then score against your policy. You get a report for each office and for the call centre.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Can you test online booking too?

Yes. We can check website and chat support, including response time and accuracy.

Do you cover tour operators?

Yes. Tour operators, ticketing offices and visa service agents can be included.

Do you buy real tickets?

We only buy when the scenario needs it and you approve the cost.
