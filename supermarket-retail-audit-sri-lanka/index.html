Retail is won in the aisle and at the till. We shop your stores as normal customers and report on stock, prices, staff and checkout across every outlet.

What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Shelf availability

Whether advertised and popular items are on the shelf, in the right place.

02

Price tag accuracy

Shelf prices matched against what the till charges.

03

Staff help

Whether staff offer help and can find a product or answer a question.

04

Checkout speed and billing

Queue length, number of open tills, and bill accuracy.

05

Store cleanliness

Floors, fresh food areas, trolleys, washrooms and signage.

06

Returns and complaints

How a customer with a faulty or wrong item is treated.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

A price mismatch or a missing promoted item loses trust fast. In a chain, each store drifts in its own way. Regular audits find those drifts early and show which managers need support.

How it works

From brief to report

We set a shopping list and a few service scenarios. Auditors shop each store, record everything on the same sheet, and report store by store. Repeat monthly to see which stores improve.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.
