Pharmacy Mystery Shopping in Sri Lanka

A pharmacy customer trusts staff with their health. We check whether staff ask the right questions, give clear advice and protect privacy, across every outlet in your chain.

What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Greeting and wait time

How quickly the customer is attended to.

02

Advice quality

Whether staff ask questions before recommending, and refer to a pharmacist when needed.

03

Prescription handling

Whether prescriptions are checked and instructions explained.

Privacy and cleanliness

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Pharmacy quality depends on the person behind the counter. A chain needs the same careful behaviour in every outlet. Mystery shopping shows whether your standard is followed when no one from head office is watching.

How it works

From brief to report

We design scenarios such as a customer asking for advice on a common ailment. Auditors visit each outlet, follow the scenario, and report against your policy and standards.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Do you test whether staff follow prescription rules?

We can design scenarios that check whether staff ask the right questions, in line with your policy. We do not buy restricted medicines.

Can you cover independent pharmacies in a franchise?

Yes. Franchise outlets can be included.

Can you check delivery services?

Yes. We can test phone and app orders and delivery.
