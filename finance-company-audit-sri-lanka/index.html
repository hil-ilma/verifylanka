What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Loan and lease enquiry

How staff handle questions on vehicle leasing, personal loans and business loans.

02

Rate and fee disclosure

Whether interest rates, fees and total cost are stated clearly and correctly.

03

Gold loan and pawning counters

Valuation explanation, paperwork accuracy, security and customer comfort.

04

Document checklist accuracy

Whether customers are told the correct documents on the first visit.

05

Fixed deposit service

Clear explanation of rates, terms and withdrawal rules.

06

Branch environment

Waiting areas, privacy at counters, and general presentation.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

A wrong answer about a rate or a document list creates repeat visits and angry customers. A pushy sales approach creates complaints. Checking from the customer side shows where the process breaks and where training is needed.

How it works

From brief to report

We plan realistic enquiry scenarios, send auditors to your branches across the island, and score each against your standards. The report shows each branch, plus common problems across the network.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Do auditors apply for real loans?

No. They make enquiries only. We do not create real financial commitments.

Can you test collection or recovery conduct?

We can design phone and counter scenarios that check how customers are spoken to. Tell us what you want tested and we will agree a safe approach.

Do you cover small towns?

Yes. We cover every district, including smaller towns where finance branches are common.
