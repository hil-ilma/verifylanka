What we check

What our auditors look at

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

A single bad night can turn into a public review that stays online for years. A mystery stay shows what your guests see in housekeeping, at the front desk and in the restaurant, including the things managers rarely notice.

How it works

From brief to report

We book a stay as a normal guest, follow a scenario, and score every step against your standard. Reports cover each department, and we can repeat visits across your whole chain of hotels, guesthouses and villas.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Do you cover resorts outside Colombo?

Yes. We cover coastal, hill country, cultural triangle and northern destinations as well as the cities.

Do you book real stays?

Yes. A mystery stay is booked and paid at a normal rate, so staff treat the auditor as a guest.

Can you check several hotels in one chain?

Yes. We can compare every property against the same score sheet.
