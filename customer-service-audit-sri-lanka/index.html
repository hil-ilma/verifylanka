Customer Service Audit in Sri Lanka

A customer service audit compares what your policy says with what your customers actually get. We test the real experience across your branches, phone lines and online channels and score it against your own standards.

Request an Audit info@verifylanka.com
  • All island coverage
  • Reports in 48 hours
  • 100% independent
What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Walk in service

Greeting, helpfulness, patience and problem solving at counters and on the shop floor.

02

Phone service

How quickly calls are picked up, whether the script is followed and whether the problem is solved on the first call.

03

Email and chat replies

Response time, tone, accuracy and whether the customer needs to ask twice.

04

Complaint handling

What happens when a customer raises a problem: who listens, who owns it, and whether it is closed.

05

Language service

Whether customers can be served in Sinhala, Tamil and English where you promise it.

06

Consistency across locations

Which branches match your standard and which fall below it.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Customers who have a poor experience seldom tell you. They quietly go to a competitor, or they tell ten friends. An audit gives you the problem before it costs you the customer, and shows you which team to train.

How it works

From brief to report

We agree the standards and scenarios with you, run the checks over a set period, and score every location. The report shows the island wide picture first, then each branch, with practical fixes ranked by impact.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

What is the difference between a service audit and mystery shopping?

Mystery shopping is the main method, and a service audit is the wider study. An audit can also include phone, email and chat checks and a review against your written standards.

Can you measure against our own standards?

Yes. We score against your policies and service promises, so the report matches how your team already works.

Do you cover places outside Colombo?

Yes. We cover the whole island, including smaller towns and remote areas.
