Competitor Benchmarking and Local Market Intelligence in Sri Lanka

Your customers compare you with the shop or branch down the road. We run the same customer scenario at your outlets and at your competitors, and show you where you are ahead and where you are behind.

What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Service comparison

Greeting, waiting time, staff knowledge and helpfulness, scored on the same sheet for every brand.

02

Prices and promotions

What a customer is actually quoted and offered at the counter, on the shelf and on the phone.

03

Product range and availability

Which products, plans or packages are offered and whether they are in stock.

04

Sales approach

How staff explain, recommend and close, and what they say about other brands.

05

Outlet standards

Location, appearance, cleanliness, signage and customer facilities.

06

Phone and online channels

Response time and quality on hotlines, websites, chat and social media.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

A score of 80 percent means little on its own. If your nearest competitor scores 90, you have a problem. If they score 60, you have an advantage to protect. Local, on the ground information shows which one it is, town by town.

How it works

From brief to report

You name the competitors and the areas. Our auditors act as ordinary customers, run the same scenario at each brand, and record only what any customer can see and hear. You get a side by side report.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Is competitor mystery shopping ethical?

Yes. Auditors behave as ordinary customers and record only what any member of the public would see and hear. We do not collect confidential information.

Will you share our results with other companies?

No. Your report is yours. We do not share client results with anyone else.

Which sectors can you benchmark?

Any sector with customer facing outlets, including banks, finance companies, telecom, retail, hotels, restaurants and vehicle showrooms.
