Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Delivery is the part of the experience you do not control directly, and it often decides whether a customer orders again. Testing real orders shows where the promise breaks.

How it works

From brief to report

We place orders from different areas of Sri Lanka, record each step and compare actual delivery with what was promised. We include a return or support request when you want it tested.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.
