Become a Mystery Shopper in Sri Lanka

Freelance work in your own area. Visit businesses as a customer, tell us what happened, and get paid for each completed assignment.

How it works

From application to payment

01

Apply by email

Send us your name, district, phone number and the languages you speak.

02

Get a brief

When a job comes up near you, we send the place, the scenario and the checklist.

03

Make the visit

Go as a normal customer. Watch, listen and remember. Do not tell staff who you are.

04

Send your report

Fill in the checklist honestly on the same day, while it is fresh.

05

Get paid

You are paid a fixed fee for each completed assignment.

06

Stay in the pool

Good, reliable shoppers are offered more assignments as new jobs come in.

The work

What a mystery shopper does

You visit a bank, shop, hotel, restaurant or other business as an ordinary customer. You follow a simple scenario, such as asking about a product, and note how you were treated. Afterwards you fill in a checklist.

We are building a pool of freelance mystery shoppers in every district of Sri Lanka, so there is work in cities and in small towns.

How to apply

Email info@verifylanka.com with the subject "Mystery shopper application" and tell us:

Questions

Common questions

Do I have to pay to join?

No. Verify Lanka never asks shoppers for a joining fee or a deposit. If someone asks you for money in our name, it is not us.

Is this a full time job?

No. It is freelance work. Assignments are offered when a job comes up in your area, and you choose which ones to take.

Which districts are you looking for shoppers in?

Every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and rural areas.

Which languages do I need?

Sinhala, Tamil or English. One language is enough.

What do I need?

You must be 18 or older, have a smartphone, and be honest, observant and on time.
