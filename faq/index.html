Mystery Shopping in Sri Lanka: Questions and Answers

Plain answers about mystery shoppers, customer service audits and how Verify Lanka works.

What is mystery shopping?

Mystery shopping is a way to measure customer service. A trained person visits a business as an ordinary customer, follows a planned scenario, and reports what happened against a checklist. Staff do not know the visit is a check.

What is a mystery shopping service?

A mystery shopping service is a company that sends trained people to visit your business as ordinary customers, then reports on the service they received. Verify Lanka is a mystery shopping service that does this for businesses across Sri Lanka, in person, by phone and online.

What is a mystery shopper?

A mystery shopper is the person who makes the visit. They are also called secret shoppers, mystery guests when they visit hotels and restaurants, and mystery callers when they test phone lines.

What is a customer service audit?

A customer service audit, also called a customer experience or CX audit, compares the service your customers really get with the service your company promises. Mystery shopping is the main method, often with phone, email and chat checks added.

Which businesses use mystery shopping in Sri Lanka?

Banks, finance and leasing companies, insurers, telecom providers, supermarkets and retail chains, hotels, restaurants, fuel stations, hospitals, pharmacies, vehicle showrooms, schools, public offices, online stores and call centres.

Which parts of Sri Lanka do you cover?

The whole island. We cover every district, including smaller towns and remote areas.

How much does mystery shopping cost in Sri Lanka?

The price depends on the number of outlets, where they are and how detailed the scenario is. Send us your outlet list and we will reply with a quote within 24 hours.

How fast will we get the report?

Within 48 hours of the visit.

What is in the report?

A score for each area checked, such as staff conduct, service speed, compliance and environment, an overall score, and written notes on what the auditor saw and heard. For several outlets you also get a ranking.

Will our staff know they are being checked?

No. Auditors behave as normal customers and do not reveal themselves. Results go only to the people you name.

Is mystery shopping fair to staff?

Yes, when it is done properly. Auditors act as ordinary customers, never try to trick staff into breaking rules, and score against standards the company has already set. Most clients use the results for training and recognition.

Can you run a one time audit, or only ongoing programmes?

Both. You can start with a single audit and move to monthly or quarterly visits later.

Can you compare us with our competitors?

Yes. We can run the same scenario at competitor outlets and report the difference.

Which languages do you work in?

Sinhala, Tamil and English.

How do I become a mystery shopper in Sri Lanka?

We take on freelance mystery shoppers in every district. See our Become a Mystery Shopper page for how to apply.
