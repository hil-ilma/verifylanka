Bank Branch Audit in Sri Lanka

Bank customers rarely complain. They move their money. Our auditors visit your branches as ordinary customers and report how tellers, officers and security staff really treat them.

What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Teller and counter service

Greeting, courtesy, accuracy and speed at deposits, withdrawals and payments.

02

Product knowledge

How well staff explain accounts, loans, cards and fixed deposits, including fees.

03

Queue and token system

Waiting time, token system use and how crowding is handled.

04

Digital banking help

Whether staff can help customers with the app, internet banking and card problems.

05

ATM and CDM area

Cleanliness, working machines, safety and signs.

06

KYC and fee disclosure

Whether staff ask for the right documents and state charges clearly.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Branch quality changes with the manager and the shift. Head office sees averages, but a customer lives one visit at a time. A branch audit shows which branches protect your reputation and which put customers at risk of leaving.

How it works

From brief to report

We choose scenarios such as opening an account, asking about a personal loan or reporting a lost card. Auditors visit each branch, score the same checklist, and you receive a ranked report within 48 hours.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Can you audit all our branches across the island?

Yes. We cover every district, so you can include city and rural branches in one programme.

Can you check ATMs and digital channels too?

Yes. We can add the ATM and CDM area, the mobile app help desk and phone banking.

Do you ask for personal account details?

No. Auditors do not share real account data. They use scenarios that do not put customers or the bank at risk.
