About Verify Lanka

An independent mystery shopping and customer experience audit company, working across all of Sri Lanka.

Who we are

A Sri Lankan mystery shopping company

Verify Lanka is a Sri Lankan mystery shopping service and customer experience audit company. We send independent auditors into businesses as ordinary customers, in person, by phone and online, and report exactly what happened.

Verify Lanka (Pvt) Ltd is a private limited company registered in Sri Lanka, company number PV 00377171. We are based in Kandy and serve businesses across the whole island.

Our only job is to show you what your customers really experience, so you can fix what is broken and keep what works.

What we do

Audits from the customer side

Mystery shopping, customer service audits, branch audits, compliance checks, competitor benchmarking and monthly tracking programmes. One outlet or a full network, anywhere on the island.

We work in Sinhala, Tamil and English, and we cover every district of Sri Lanka.

How we work

Six rules we do not bend

01

Independent

We work for the owner or head office, never for the branch being checked. Our auditors have no link to the outlets they visit.

02

Unannounced

Staff are not told when a visit will happen. What we see is an ordinary day.

03

Evidence, not opinion

Every score comes from a fixed checklist and written notes of what was seen and heard.

04

Confidential

Findings go only to the people you name. We do not share client results with anyone else.

05

The same standard everywhere

One score sheet for every outlet, so a branch in Jaffna is measured the same way as a branch in Colombo.

06

Fast

Reports reach you within 48 hours of the visit, while there is still time to act.
