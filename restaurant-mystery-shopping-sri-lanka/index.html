Restaurant and Cafe Mystery Shopping in Sri Lanka

In food service, the experience repeats at every outlet, and it only takes one bad visit to lose a regular. We eat, order and collect as normal customers and report what we find.

What we check

What our auditors look at

01

Welcome and seating

Greeting, waiting time and how quickly a table is offered.

02

Menu knowledge

Whether staff can describe dishes, ingredients and recommend choices.

03

Order accuracy

Whether what arrives is what was ordered, and whether it arrives together.

04

Food quality and temperature

Taste, presentation, freshness and serving temperature.

05

Hygiene

Tables, cutlery, staff handling, washrooms and kitchen visibility.

06

Bill and takeaway or delivery

Bill accuracy, packaging, delivery time and condition on arrival.

Why it matters

What you cannot see from head office

Customers compare your outlets with each other, whether you want them to or not. A chain is judged by its weakest branch. Regular checks keep every outlet at the same standard.

How it works

From brief to report

Auditors dine in, take away or order delivery, using fixed scenarios. Each outlet is scored on the same sheet, so you can compare outlets and track the effect of training.

We cover every district of Sri Lanka, including smaller towns and remote areas, and we can work in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

Questions

Common questions

Do you pay for the meals?

Meal costs are included in the agreed audit fee or billed at cost, depending on the plan we agree with you.

Can you check delivery and takeaway?

Yes. We can test your own delivery and delivery apps.

Do you check food safety?

We record what a customer can see, such as cleanliness and handling. We do not replace an official food safety inspection.
